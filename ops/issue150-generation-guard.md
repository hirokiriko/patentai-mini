# Issue 150: retain complete conditions in representative quotations

The first approved follow-up comparison stopped during generation validation.
The nominal reservation grammar rejected an otherwise permitted noun phrase
joined with `又は`. Separately, some generated observations omitted conditions,
and the fixed quote partitions could separate a condition from its action.
Those are distinct defects: accepting a valid nominal reservation does not
establish that the remaining prose is faithful to the original.

The correction accepts `又は` only between independently valid nominal topics.
Embedded assertions, predicates and conditions remain invalid reservations.
Generation instructions require each observation to retain the subject,
conditions and action of its selected representative fact. They do not treat
one quotation as proof of every assertion in the comparison.

Additional quote choices are continuous slices of the unchanged original,
derived from the action and guard positions of successfully decoded source
facts. They retain the original claim, source and occurrence, and remain within
the existing 120-character limit. Original partitions remain available,
including when no additional slice fits. The transport, schema and decoder
must use the same choices. These positions are evidence for human and target
review, not proof of complete Japanese semantic understanding.

The source answers and failed generated answer are retained without repair.
Existing saved v1 results, authentication and export formats remain unchanged.
The previous adjacent-premise quotation fix and its omission, ordering and
wrong-occurrence rejections remain in force.
